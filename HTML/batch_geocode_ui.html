Batch Geocode Learners

Automatically add precise coordinates to learner addresses

How it works

This tool will automatically find the exact coordinates (latitude and longitude) for each learner based on their address. This enables:

  • Precise pins on the map instead of approximate barangay centers
  • Sitio-level visualization
  • Better enrollment planning and outreach

Note: This process takes about 1 second per learner to respect the geocoding service rate limits.
